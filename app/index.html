Detailed results

Architecture

Deep localiser

Per-service LSTM encoders read 30-second metric windows; directed graph attention passes evidence along the observed call graph; a root head ranks services. Trained on known incidents only.

Pattern memory

Each incident becomes a signature centred on its origin service. Nearest-neighbour similarity decides known vs. new; the threshold is set on validation data so 95 % of known incidents are accepted.

Pattern discovery

Unclassified incidents are clustered (HDBSCAN). An engineer names a group and it becomes a new pattern immediately, with no retraining.

Blast radius forecast

PropagationNet, a graph neural network with a discrete-time hazard head, estimates for every service the chance and time of impact, and updates every 10 seconds.
